    FOR THE GROWN-UP

    What this game will not do to your child

    NO
    No open chat, no strangers, no direct messages. There is no channel through which an adult can reach a child inside PAIX. None is planned.
    NO
    No image or video upload, no camera, no microphone. Nothing can be sent in, so nothing indecent can be pushed at a child or extracted from one.
    NO
    No advertising, no third-party trackers, no outbound links. The world is closed. There is no click that leads out of it.
    NO
    No infinite scroll, no streaks, no loot boxes, no timers that punish leaving. Nothing here is engineered to be hard to stop.
    NO
    No accounts sold, no child data brokered. Progress is stored on the device. There is nothing to harvest.
    YES
    URIEL stops the game every twelve minutes and stands up. The Stand-Up is not skippable and it is not a penalty. It is the posture rule, enforced by the narrator rather than by a parent.
